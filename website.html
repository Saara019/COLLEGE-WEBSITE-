ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Smt. Shardaben Champaklal Nanavati Institute of Polytechnic

Established in 1984

The Smt. Shardaben Champaklal Nanavati Institute of Polytechnic was established in 1984 and has completed four decades of dedicated service in education for women students.

The Polytechnic fulfills the growing need for vocation-oriented, skill-based courses that also encourage entrepreneurship — especially important for young women who may not have the opportunity to pursue full-fledged careers outside their homes.

For over 42 years, our institution has been at the forefront of women's education, providing industry-relevant skills and fostering excellence, innovation, and empowerment.

Our Parent Body — Seva Mandal Education Society

Seva Mandal Education Society is committed to the vision of 'Empowerment of Women through Quality in Education'. The Society strives to impart qualitative education to all students, irrespective of caste, class, and religion.

Today, Seva Mandal Education Society is recognized as one of the foremost educational campuses in Mumbai, offering specialization in a multitude of courses geared for the young Indian women of the new generation.

Admission Process

Join Our Institute

Take the first step towards a fulfilling creative career. Our admission process is simple, transparent, and student-friendly.

Step-by-Step Admission Procedure

1

Application Submission

Visit the institute and collect / fill the application form. Submit with:

  • Last Qualifying Exam (Original)
  • Qualifying Marksheets — HSC / SSC / UG (Original)
  • Migration Certificate — Board or University (Photocopy)
  • Gap Certificate
  • Caste Certificate
  • Non-Creame Layer Certificate (OBC,VJNT,SBC)
  • Disability Certificate (Where Applicable)
2

Fee Payment

Fee can be paid via demand draft, or online transfer. Seat is confirmed only after fee payment.

Important Notice

Schedule: Admissions open June to August every year.

Reservations: SC / ST / OBC / EWS government norms are strictly followed.(Not Applicable for MSBTE courses)

Scholarships: Various scholarships and freeships are available for eligible students.

Procedure: Admission granted to students will remain provisional until verification and approval of documents as per Government and MSBTE norms

General Eligibility Criteria

  • SSC pass for MSBTE courses
  • Valid government-issued identity proof
  • Valid Email-Id, Aadhar Card linked with valid phone number
  • 4 recent passport-sized photographs

About Seva Mandal Education Society

Seva Mandal Education Society has been a pioneer in providing qualitative educational facilities to girl students for the last six decades. We started on a humble note in 1957 with just seven students and today our campus which is located in the central suburb of Matunga houses eight institutions with over 6000 female students.

We offer every educational opportunity ranging from Junior college to undergraduate programs, postgraduate programs and certificate courses in faculties of Arts, Commerce, Home Science, Computer applications, Management, Mass Media, Law and Nursing.

Our vision statement is the "Empowerment of Women through Quality in Education" and it is our objective to ensure that female students who would have otherwise been denied the opportunity for education are given the chance to pursue their dreams.
